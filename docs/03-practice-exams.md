## Library — findings (researched 2026-11-17)

**Bottom line: no Arizona library database found with an AZ ROC or KB-2 practice test.** Library test-prep databases (LearningExpress, Peterson's) cover GED, SAT, ASVAB, CDL, nursing and similar exams, but not state contractor trade exams. Where libraries do help is with physical copies of the study-only books.

| Library | What it has | Use for KB-2 |
|---|---|---|
| Phoenix Public Library | Peterson's Test and Career Prep (free with a card) | General test-taking only, no ROC content |
| Mesa CC Library | Databases and eResources (MEID login) | Possible reserve copies of construction texts; ask at the desk |
| ASU Libraries | Engineering and construction collection | Most likely place to find PCA *Design and Control of Concrete Mixtures*, *Modern Masonry*, *Carpentry and Building Construction* |
| WorldCat (worldcat.org) | Finds which libraries hold a book | Search each study-only title by ISBN to locate copies in the Valley |

Edition warning: PCA's *Design and Control of Concrete Mixtures* is now on its **17th edition**. The exam accepts the **2011 (15th) or 2016 (16th)** edition. Library and bookstore copies may be the wrong one.

## College and university — findings (researched 2026-11-17)

**Bottom line: no Arizona college or university offers an AZ ROC license-prep course.** The closest school-based option is the **Arizona School of Real Estate & Business** (a private school), which runs a 16-hour trade exam prep course. Colleges do offer credit courses that match the exam domains. They run a full semester, so they're useful for building the deeper guide later, not for this week.

| School | Courses matching the exam blueprint | Domains |
|---|---|---|
| Mesa Community College (Construction Management/Trades, Dr. David Cain, 480-461-7377) | CON106 Foundations of Concrete; CON140 Masonry; CON110–112 floor, wall and roof framing; CON213 Steel Framing; CON214 Roof Systems and Building Envelopes; CON215 Interior Finishes; CON216 Concrete Forms; CON103/CON271 Construction Safety; Construction Safety OSHA 30 certificate | Nearly all 10 domains |
| GateWay CC / South Mountain CC | Construction Management and Construction Technology certificates and AAS | General |
| ASU Del E. Webb School of Construction | CON 252 Building Construction Methods, Materials and Equipment; CON 383 Estimating; open to non-degree students | Concrete, Metals, Masonry, Carpentry |

Collegiate-level text worth knowing: *Construction Materials, Methods, and Techniques* (Spence and Kultermann) is the standard materials-and-methods textbook in college syllabi. It covers the same ground as the study-only books.

## Conclusion

For a near-term test date, the best free tools are **OpenExamPrep's question bank** plus the **free online IBC, IRC and OSHA 1926** for lookup drills. Libraries and colleges help the longer-term guide build-out, not this week.
