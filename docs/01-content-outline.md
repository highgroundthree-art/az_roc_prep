## How to use this

- The top four domains (Concrete, Metals, Carpentry, Safety) are **51%** of the exam.
- Safety and General Building Code (21 items) come straight from books you can bring in, so tab them well and those points come from lookup speed.
- Concrete, Metals, Masonry and Sitework draw on **study-only** books. Those points depend on what you actually know.
- The "primary reference" column is a working guess at chapter mapping and should be checked as each domain folder is built.
