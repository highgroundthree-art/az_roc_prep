# Official Sources — The State's "Standards"

Arizona doesn't publish an IDE-style standards document for contractors. The equivalent is the **PSI Candidate Information Bulletin (CIB)** plus the **trade-specific content outline** it links to. Together they define what's tested, the item counts, and which references are allowed.

## Primary (authoritative)

| Source | What it gives you | Link |

| PSI Arizona Contractor CIB | Exam process, fees, retake rules, reference rules, links to content outlines | https://test-takers.psiexams.com/api/content/bulletin/5617 |

## Key rules from the CIB and AZ ROC

- B-1, B-2, KB-1 and KB-2 were combined into **one trade exam** (effective 6/20/2024).
- The SRE is required for new applicants who haven't been a qualifying party on an Arizona license in the last five years. It is online only through Gmetrix and cannot be waived.
- Retakes: wait 30 days after a failed attempt. After 3 failures, wait 90 days before each further retake.
- Scores are valid for 2 years. The application and score report must reach AZ ROC within that window.
- Reference books may be highlighted, underlined and tabbed with **permanent** tabs only. No writing during the exam.

## Exam waiver: check this first

A.R.S. § 32-1122(F): the registrar **shall waive** the examination requirement if ROC records show the qualifying party "is currently or has previously been a qualifying party for a licensee in this state in the same classification within the preceding five years." Subsection E.1 also waives work-experience documentation on the same basis.

- Statute: https://www.azleg.gov/ars/32/01122.htm
- Rule: A.A.C. R4-9-106(E)
- Form: AZ ROC In-State Waiver Request form (roc.az.gov)
- Confirm with ROC Licensing at (602) 542-1525 before paying for or sitting an exam.
