# Doors & Windows — 5 items

Main sources: IRC R308 (safety glazing), R310 (emergency escape), R311 (egress doors), R312.2 (window fall protection), R302.5 (garage doors), Ch. 6 (headers, installation); IBC 1010 (doors), 2406 (safety glazing). Background: *Carpentry and Building Construction* (installation).

## Key concepts

**Emergency escape and rescue openings (IRC R310)**
| Requirement | Value |
|---|---|
| Net clear opening | 5.7 sq ft (5.0 sq ft at grade floor or below grade) |
| Min clear height | 24 in |
| Min clear width | 20 in |
| Max sill height | 44 in above floor |
| Where required | Basements, habitable attics, every sleeping room |
| Window wells | 9 sq ft min area, 36 in min projection; ladder if deeper than 44 in |

**Egress doors**
- IRC: at least one egress door, 32 in clear width, 78 in high, side-hinged (R311.2).
- IBC: 32 in clear width, 80 in high (1010.1.1).
- IBC: doors must swing in the direction of egress travel for an occupant load of 50 or more, or in Group H (1010.1.2.1).
- IBC: panic or fire exit hardware is required in Group A and E with an occupant load of 50 or more (1010.1.10).

**Safety glazing: hazardous locations (IRC R308.4 / IBC 2406.4)**
1. Glazing in all doors (fixed or operable panels).
2. Within a 24 in arc of either vertical door edge, in the closed position, with the bottom edge less than 60 in above the floor.
3. Large individual panes: all four conditions must apply. Area over 9 sq ft; bottom edge less than 18 in above the floor; top edge more than 36 in above the floor; a walking surface within 36 in horizontally.
4. Guards and railings.
5. Wet areas: walls and enclosures of tubs, showers, hot tubs and saunas, where the bottom edge is less than 60 in above the standing surface.
6. Near pools and spas.
7. Adjacent to stairs and ramps: bottom edge less than 36 in above the walking surface.
8. At the bottom of a stair landing: within 60 in horizontally of the bottom tread, with the bottom edge less than 36 in above the walking surface.

**Window fall protection (IRC R312.2).** Where the opening is more than 72 in above finished grade, the sill must be at least 24 in above the floor, or the window needs an opening-limiting device or fall prevention device (ASTM F2090).

**Garage-to-dwelling door (IRC R302.5.1).** Solid wood or solid- or honeycomb-core steel at least 1⅜ in thick, or a 20-minute fire-rated door. No openings are allowed from a garage directly into a sleeping room. Check R302.5.1 in your book for whether a self-closing device is required.

**Fenestration energy.** NFRC labels show U-factor and SHGC. Zone 2: U-0.40, SHGC 0.25.

**Installation**
- Apply the sill pan or flashing first, then the jambs, then the head flashing on top, lapped over the WRB, all shingle-fashion.
- Rough opening is typically the unit size plus about ½ in each way.
- Headers carry the load over the opening, with jack (trimmer) studs and king studs at each side.

## Practice questions

### D1
Under the IRC, an emergency escape and rescue opening must have a minimum net clear opening of ___ (except at grade floor openings).
- A) 4.4 sq ft
- B) 5.0 sq ft
- C) 5.7 sq ft
- D) 6.0 sq ft

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 5.7 sq ft (5.0 sq ft for grade-floor or below-grade openings). *Ref:* IRC R310.2.1

</details>

### D2
The sill of an emergency escape and rescue window may be no higher than ___ above the floor.
- A) 36 in
- B) 42 in
- C) 44 in
- D) 48 in

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 44 in. *Ref:* IRC R310.2.2

</details>

### D3
Minimum net clear height and width for an emergency escape opening are:
- A) 20 in high / 24 in wide
- B) 24 in high / 20 in wide
- C) 22 in high / 22 in wide
- D) 24 in high / 24 in wide

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — 24 in high, 20 in wide. *Ref:* IRC R310.2.1

</details>

### D4
The required egress door for a dwelling must provide a clear width of at least ___ and a clear height of at least 78 in.
- A) 30 in
- B) 32 in
- C) 34 in
- D) 36 in

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — 32 in. *Ref:* IRC R311.2

</details>

### D5
Glazing adjacent to a door is a hazardous location when it is within a ___ arc of either vertical edge of the door (closed) and its bottom edge is less than 60 in above the floor.
- A) 12 in
- B) 18 in
- C) 24 in
- D) 36 in

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 24 in. *Ref:* IRC R308.4.2; IBC 2406.4.2

</details>

### D6
A large fixed window requires safety glazing only when ALL of these apply: pane larger than 9 sq ft, bottom edge less than 18 in above the floor, top edge more than 36 in above the floor, and:
- A) It faces south
- B) A walking surface is within 36 in horizontally
- C) It is within 10 ft of a door
- D) It is double-pane

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B**. *Ref:* IRC R308.4.3; IBC 2406.4.3

</details>

### D7
Glazing in walls enclosing a bathtub or shower is a hazardous location when its bottom edge is less than ___ above the standing or walking surface.
- A) 36 in
- B) 48 in
- C) 60 in
- D) 72 in

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 60 in. *Ref:* IRC R308.4.5

</details>

### D8
A door between a dwelling and an attached garage must be solid wood or steel at least 1⅜ in thick, or:
- A) A 20-minute fire-rated door
- B) A 45-minute fire-rated door
- C) A 1-hour fire-rated door
- D) Any exterior door

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**A** — 20-minute fire-rated. *Ref:* IRC R302.5.1

</details>

### D9
Under the IBC, egress doors must swing in the direction of egress travel when serving a room or area with an occupant load of:
- A) 10 or more
- B) 30 or more
- C) 50 or more
- D) 100 or more

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 50 or more (or any Group H). *Ref:* IBC 1010.1.2.1

</details>

### D10
In a dwelling, where an operable window opening is more than 72 in above finished grade, the lowest part of the clear opening must be at least ___ above the floor unless a fall prevention or opening-limiting device is installed.
- A) 18 in
- B) 24 in
- C) 30 in
- D) 36 in

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — 24 in. *Ref:* IRC R312.2.1

</details>

### D11
The performance label on windows and doors showing U-factor and SHGC comes from:
- A) ANSI
- B) NFRC
- C) UL
- D) AAMA

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — National Fenestration Rating Council. *Ref:* IRC N1101.10.3

</details>

### D12
Under the IBC, panic or fire exit hardware is required on swinging egress doors with locks or latches in Group A and E occupancies with an occupant load of:
- A) 10 or more
- B) 30 or more
- C) 50 or more
- D) 300 or more

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 50 or more. *Ref:* IBC 1010.1.10

</details>
