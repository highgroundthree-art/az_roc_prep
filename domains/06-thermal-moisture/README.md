# Thermal & Moisture Protection — 10 items

These questions are answerable from the books in the room: IRC Ch. 7 (wall covering), Ch. 8 (attic ventilation), Ch. 9 (roofing), Ch. 11 / N11 (energy), R406 (dampproofing); IBC Ch. 14 (exterior walls), Ch. 15 (roofing), Ch. 7 (fire-resistance and firestopping).

## Key concepts

**Vapor retarder classes (IRC R702.7 / IBC 1404.3)**
| Class | Permeance | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I | 0.1 perm or less | Polyethylene sheet, foil |
| II | Over 0.1 up to 1.0 perm | Kraft-faced batts |
| III | Over 1.0 up to 10 perm | Latex paint |

**Phoenix and the Valley are in Climate Zone 2B** (hot-dry). IRC Table N1102.1.2:
- Ceiling: R-38
- Wood-frame wall: R-13
- Floor: R-13
- Fenestration: U-0.40, SHGC 0.25

**Attic ventilation (IRC R806)**
- Net free vent area: 1/150 of the vented space.
- 1/300 is allowed if 40–50% of the venting is in the upper portion (within 3 ft of the ridge).
- Vent openings: protected with screens of 1/16 to 1/4 in.
- Attic access: 22 × 30 in minimum (R807).

**Roofing slope minimums**
| Covering | Min slope | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Asphalt shingles | 2:12 | Double underlayment from 2:12 to under 4:12 (IRC R905.2.2) |
| Clay or concrete tile | 2½:12 | Double underlayment from 2½:12 to under 4:12 (IRC R905.3.2) |
| Built-up roof (BUR) | ¼:12 | Coal-tar BUR ⅛:12 (IBC 1507.10.1) |
| Wood shingles / shakes | 3:12 | IRC R905.7.1, R905.8.2 |

**Roofing details**
- Drip edge: lap 2 in, nail max 12 in o.c. (R905.2.8.5).
- Chimney cricket: needed when the chimney is over 30 in wide parallel to the ridge (R903.2.2).
- Re-covering: not allowed over two or more existing layers (R908.3.1.1).
- Asphalt shingles: 4 fasteners per strip shingle, more in high-wind areas (R905.2.6).

**Walls**
- A water-resistive barrier (#15 felt or approved) goes behind the siding (R703.2).
- Flashing laps shingle-fashion, upper over lower: at windows and doors, wall-roof intersections, and beneath the first course of masonry (R703.4).

**Stucco**
- Three coats over lath, about ⅞ in total (R703.7).
- Weep screed at the foundation plate line: at least 4 in above earth or 2 in above paved areas (R703.7.2.1).
- Two layers of Grade D paper over wood-based sheathing (R703.7.3).

**Below grade**
- Dampproofing: foundation walls that retain earth and enclose interior spaces below grade (R406.1).
- Waterproofing: where there's a high water table or severe soil-water conditions (R406.2).

**Firestopping vs fireblocking**
- Firestopping: rated penetration and joint systems in fire-resistance-rated assemblies (IBC 714, 715).
- Fireblocking: blocking concealed draft openings in combustible construction (IBC 718; IRC R302.11).

**OSHA:** a low-slope roof is 4:12 or less (1926.500(b)).

## Practice questions

### T1
A Class I vapor retarder has a permeance of:
- A) 0.1 perm or less
- B) Over 0.1 up to 1.0 perm
- C) Over 1.0 up to 10 perm
- D) Over 10 perm

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**A**. *Ref:* IRC R202 definitions / R702.7

</details>

### T2
The minimum net free ventilating area for an enclosed attic is ___ of the area of the vented space, unless the conditions for a reduced ratio are met.
- A) 1/100
- B) 1/150
- C) 1/300
- D) 1/500

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — 1/150 (1/300 permitted with conditions). *Ref:* IRC R806.2

</details>

### T3
Asphalt shingles may be installed on roof slopes down to:
- A) 1:12
- B) 2:12
- C) 3:12
- D) 4:12

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — 2:12. Slopes from 2:12 up to less than 4:12 require double underlayment. *Ref:* IRC R905.2.2

</details>

### T4
The minimum slope for clay or concrete roof tile is:
- A) 2:12
- B) 2½:12
- C) 3:12
- D) 4:12

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — 2½:12. *Ref:* IRC R905.3.2

</details>

### T5
A built-up roof (other than coal-tar) must have a design slope of at least:
- A) ⅛ in per ft
- B) ¼ in per ft
- C) ½ in per ft
- D) 1 in per ft

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — ¼ unit vertical in 12 (2%). *Ref:* IBC 1507.10.1

</details>

### T6
Drip edge at eaves and gables of a shingle roof must overlap at joints by at least ___ and be fastened at no more than 12 in o.c.
- A) 1 in
- B) 2 in
- C) 3 in
- D) 4 in

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — 2 in. *Ref:* IRC R905.2.8.5

</details>

### T7
A cricket or saddle is required on the ridge side of a chimney when the chimney's dimension parallel to the ridge exceeds:
- A) 18 in
- B) 24 in
- C) 30 in
- D) 36 in

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 30 in. *Ref:* IRC R903.2.2

</details>

### T8
A new roof covering may not be installed over an existing roof when the existing roof has:
- A) Any layer of wood shingles
- B) One layer of asphalt shingles
- C) Two or more applications of any type of roof covering
- D) Underlayment that is more than 10 years old

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C**. *Ref:* IRC R908.3.1.1

</details>

### T9
A stucco weep screed must be placed at least ___ above earth or 2 in above paved areas.
- A) 2 in
- B) 4 in
- C) 6 in
- D) 8 in

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — 4 in. *Ref:* IRC R703.7.2.1

</details>

### T10
Foundation walls that retain earth and enclose interior spaces below grade must, at minimum, be:
- A) Waterproofed in all cases
- B) Dampproofed
- C) Insulated to R-10
- D) Covered with drainage board only

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — Dampproofed. Waterproofing is required where a high water table or severe soil-water conditions exist. *Ref:* IRC R406.1, R406.2

</details>

### T11
Under the IRC prescriptive energy table, the minimum ceiling insulation for Climate Zone 2 (Phoenix area) is:
- A) R-30
- B) R-38
- C) R-49
- D) R-60

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — R-38. *Ref:* IRC Table N1102.1.2

</details>

### T12
In Climate Zone 2, the maximum fenestration SHGC under the IRC prescriptive path is:
- A) 0.25
- B) 0.30
- C) 0.40
- D) 0.55

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**A** — 0.25 (with a U-factor of 0.40). *Ref:* IRC Table N1102.1.2

</details>

### T13
Wall flashing should be installed:
- A) With the lower piece lapped over the upper piece
- B) Shingle-fashion, with upper layers lapped over lower layers to direct water outward
- C) Only at the top of windows
- D) Behind the framing

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B**. *Ref:* IRC R703.4

</details>

### T14
Under OSHA, a "low-slope roof" has a slope of:
- A) 2 in 12 or less
- B) 3 in 12 or less
- C) 4 in 12 or less
- D) 6 in 12 or less

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 4 in 12 or less. *Ref:* 1926.500(b)

</details>

### T15
Through-penetrations of fire-resistance-rated walls are protected by:
- A) Fireblocking of 2-in nominal lumber
- B) Draftstopping
- C) Approved through-penetration firestop systems
- D) Vapor retarders

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — Firestop systems tested to ASTM E814/UL 1479. *Ref:* IBC 714

</details>
