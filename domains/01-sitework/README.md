# Sitework — 9 items

Main source: *Pipe and Excavation Contracting* (1987 or 2011, **study only**). In-room backup: OSHA Subpart P (excavations), IRC R401 (grading and drainage), IBC Ch. 18 and Appendix J (grading), IRC Ch. 30 / plumbing sewer slopes.

## Key concepts

**Soil and earthwork**
- Bank (in-place) volume is what's in the ground. Loose volume is after digging (it swells). Compacted volume is after compaction (it shrinks).
- Loose volume = bank volume × (1 + swell %).
- Typical swell: sand and gravel 10–15%; common earth 20–30%; clay 30–40%; rock 50% or more.

**Compaction**
- Lab test: Proctor (standard ASTM D698, modified ASTM D1557), which sets maximum dry density at optimum moisture.
- Field tests: nuclear density gauge, sand cone.
- Specs are usually 90–95% relative compaction.

**Compaction equipment**
- Sheepsfoot or padfoot roller: cohesive soils (clay).
- Vibratory smooth-drum roller or plate compactor: granular soils (sand, gravel).
- Pneumatic (rubber-tired) roller: mixed soils, proof-rolling.

**Earthwork volume: average end area method**
- V (cu yd) = [(A1 + A2) ÷ 2 × L] ÷ 27, with areas in sq ft and length in ft.

**Surveying**
- Height of instrument (HI) = benchmark elevation + backsight.
- Elevation = HI − foresight.
- Grade stakes are marked C (cut) or F (fill) with the depth.

**Pipe**
- Bedding supports the bottom of the pipe. Haunching fills the zone from the bedding up to the springline (mid-pipe), and it's the most important zone for flexible pipe. Initial backfill runs to about 12 in over the crown.
- Rigid pipe: RCP, VCP, DIP (semi). Flexible pipe: PVC, HDPE.
- Thrust blocks go at bends, tees, caps and valves on pressure pipe.
- Building sewer slope (IRC P3005.3 / IPC 704.1): 2½ in or less at ¼ in/ft; 3–6 in at ⅛ in/ft.

**Dewatering.** Sumps and pumps; wellpoints for granular soils; deep wells.

**Arizona specifics**
- **Arizona 811 (Blue Stake):** call at least **2 full working days** before you excavate (A.R.S. § 40-360.22).
- Stormwater: an AZPDES Construction General Permit and SWPPP from ADEQ are required when disturbing **1 acre or more**.

**Grading and drainage (IRC R401.3)**
- Fall of at least 6 in within the first 10 ft from the foundation (5%).
- Impervious surfaces within 10 ft: at least 2% slope away.

**IBC Appendix J (grading, where adopted)**
- Cut and fill slopes no steeper than 2H:1V.
- Fill compacted to 90% of maximum dry density.

## Practice questions

### X1
In Arizona, before excavating you must notify Arizona 811 (Blue Stake) at least:
- A) 24 hours in advance
- B) 2 full working days in advance
- C) 5 working days in advance
- D) 10 calendar days in advance

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — Two full working days. *Ref:* A.R.S. § 40-360.22

</details>

### X2
100 bank cubic yards of common earth with a 25% swell factor will produce about how many loose cubic yards?
- A) 75
- B) 100
- C) 125
- D) 133

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 100 × 1.25 = 125 loose cu yd.

</details>

### X3
The laboratory test used to set the maximum dry density and optimum moisture content for compaction specs is the:
- A) Slump test
- B) Proctor test
- C) Atterberg limits test
- D) Sieve analysis

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — Proctor (standard ASTM D698 or modified ASTM D1557).

</details>

### X4
The best compactor for cohesive clay soils is a:
- A) Smooth-drum vibratory roller
- B) Vibratory plate
- C) Sheepsfoot (padfoot) roller
- D) Rubber-tired loader

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — Sheepsfoot. It kneads the clay.

</details>

### X5
A benchmark elevation is 100.00 ft. The backsight on it reads 5.20 ft, and the foresight on a stake reads 3.70 ft. What is the stake elevation?
- A) 96.30 ft
- B) 98.50 ft
- C) 101.50 ft
- D) 108.90 ft

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — HI = 100.00 + 5.20 = 105.20; 105.20 − 3.70 = 101.50 ft.

</details>

### X6
Using the average end area method, two cross sections 100 ft apart have areas of 60 sq ft and 48 sq ft. The volume is:
- A) 200 cu yd
- B) 400 cu yd
- C) 5,400 cu yd
- D) 10,800 cu yd

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**A** — (60 + 48) ÷ 2 × 100 = 5,400 cu ft ÷ 27 = 200 cu yd.

</details>

### X7
Under the IRC, the lot must be graded so the ground falls at least ___ within the first 10 ft from the foundation.
- A) 2 in
- B) 4 in
- C) 6 in
- D) 12 in

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 6 in (5%). *Ref:* IRC R401.3

</details>

### X8
For flexible pipe such as PVC, the most critical zone of the pipe embedment for structural support is the:
- A) Final backfill
- B) Haunching
- C) Surface restoration
- D) Trench wall

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — Haunching (from the bedding to the springline). *Ref:* Pipe and Excavation Contracting

</details>

### X9
Thrust blocks are installed on pressurized water lines primarily at:
- A) Straight runs every 100 ft
- B) Bends, tees, caps and valves

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B**. *Ref:* Pipe and Excavation Contracting

</details>

### X10
The minimum slope for a 4-in building sewer is:
- A) 1/16 in per ft
- B) ⅛ in per ft
- C) ¼ in per ft
- D) ½ in per ft

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — ⅛ in per ft for 3 to 6 in pipe (¼ in per ft for 2½ in and smaller). *Ref:* IRC P3005.3 / IPC 704.1

</details>

### X11
An AZPDES Construction General Permit (with SWPPP) from ADEQ is required for construction that disturbs at least:
- A) ¼ acre
- B) ½ acre
- C) 1 acre
- D) 5 acres

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 1 acre (or smaller sites that are part of a larger common plan).

</details>

### X12
Where the IBC grading appendix applies, cut and fill slopes generally may not be steeper than:
- A) 1H:1V
- B) 1½H:1V
- C) 2H:1V
- D) 3H:1V

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 2 horizontal to 1 vertical. *Ref:* IBC Appendix J (J106, J107)

</details>

### X13
