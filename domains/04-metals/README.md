# Metals — 13 items

Main source: *Handling and Erection of Steel Joists and Joist Girders* (SJI, 2006 or 2008, **study only**). In-room backup: OSHA 1926 Subpart R (1926.750–.761) and IBC Ch. 22.

## Key concepts

**Joist series (SJI)**
| Series | What it is |
|---|---|
| K | Open-web steel joists, short span (up to about 60 ft) |
| LH | Longspan joists |
| DLH | Deep longspan joists |
| Joist girders | Primary members supporting joists at panel points |
| CJ | Composite joists |

**Minimum bearing (SJI)**
| Series | On steel | On masonry or concrete |
|---|---|---|
| K | 2½ in | 4 in |
| LH / DLH | 4 in | 6 in |

**End anchorage**
- K-series: two ⅛ in fillet welds 1 in long (one each side of the seat), or a ½ in bolt.
- LH/DLH: two ¼ in fillet welds 2 in long, or ¾ in bolts.

**Bridging.** Horizontal (continuous angles welded to chords) or diagonal (cross-bracing, bolted or welded). Bridging holds the chords in line and prevents lateral buckling. Bridging ends must be anchored to a wall or beam.

**Erection bridging.** SJI tables flag long or heavy joists (the red-shaded spans) that need bolted diagonal bridging installed **before the hoisting line is released**. OSHA mirrors this in 1926.757 and its Tables A and B.

**Handling**
- Lift and store joists upright, on blocking, off the ground.
- Never lift by the bridging or web members.
- Use spreader bars for long joists.
- Don't drop or drag them.

**Construction loads**
- No loads go on joists until bridging is installed and anchored and the joist ends are attached (1926.757(e)).
- Place loads within 1 ft of the secured end or over joist girders at panel points.
- Don't exceed the joist's rated capacity.
- Bundles of decking: spread them out, and land them only where the joist can carry them.

**Structural steel**
| Item | Standard |
|---|---|
| W-shapes | ASTM A992, Fy = 50 ksi |
| Angles, plates (common) | ASTM A36, Fy = 36 ksi |
| High-strength bolts | ASTM F3125 (Grades A325, A490) |
| Structural welding | AWS D1.1 |
| Steel design and specs | AISC 360 (referenced by IBC 2205) |

**OSHA Subpart R numbers**
- 4 anchor rods per column (755(a)(1)).
- 2 bolts per connection before releasing the hoisting line, wrench-tight (756(a)(1)).
- Fall protection at 15 ft (760(a)(1)).
- Connectors: protected above 2 stories or 30 ft (760(b)).
- Controlled decking zone: 15–30 ft only, max 90 ft × 90 ft (760(c)).
- Concrete or mortar must reach 75% of design strength before steel erection on footings and piers (752(a)(1)).

## Practice questions

### M1
LH-series steel joists are:
- A) Light-gauge cold-formed joists
- B) Longspan open-web joists
- C) Composite joists
- D) Joist girders

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — Longspan. *Ref:* SJI

</details>

### M2
The minimum bearing length for a K-series joist on a steel member is:
- A) 1½ in
- B) 2½ in
- C) 4 in
- D) 6 in

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — 2½ in. *Ref:* SJI K-series specification

</details>

### M3
The minimum bearing length for a K-series joist on masonry or concrete is:
- A) 2½ in
- B) 3 in
- C) 4 in
- D) 6 in

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 4 in. *Ref:* SJI K-series specification

</details>

### M4
LH- and DLH-series joists bearing on masonry or concrete require a minimum bearing of:
- A) 2½ in
- B) 4 in
- C) 6 in
- D) 8 in

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 6 in (4 in on steel). *Ref:* SJI LH/DLH specification

</details>

### M5
Steel joists should never be lifted or handled by:
- A) The top chord at panel points
- B) Spreader bars
- C) Their bridging
- D) Choker slings at the ends

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — Bridging (or web members). *Ref:* SJI handling guide

</details>

### M6
Construction loads may be placed on steel joists:
- A) As soon as the joist is set on its seat
- B) Only after bridging is installed and anchored and the joist bearing ends are attached
- C) Only after the deck is welded
- D) Once the hoisting line is released

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B**. *Ref:* 1926.757(e); SJI

</details>

### M7
For joists that require bolted diagonal erection bridging, that bridging must be installed:
- A) Before the hoisting line is released
- B) Before the end of the shift
- C) Before decking is placed
- D) Before the joists are welded

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**A**. *Ref:* 1926.757(c) and Tables A/B; SJI erection stability

</details>

### M8
Under OSHA, solid-web structural members may not be released from the hoisting line until each connection has at least:
- A) One bolt, wrench-tight
- B) Two bolts, wrench-tight
- C) Four bolts, fully pretensioned
- D) A tack weld

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — Two bolts per connection, of the size and strength shown on the erection drawings, drawn up wrench-tight. *Ref:* 1926.756(a)(1)

</details>

### M9
Each column must be anchored with a minimum of:
- A) 2 anchor rods
- B) 3 anchor rods
- C) 4 anchor rods
- D) 6 anchor rods

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 4. *Ref:* 1926.755(a)(1)

</details>

### M10

</details>
