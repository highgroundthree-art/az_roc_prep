# Finishes — 6 items

Main sources: IRC R702 (interior covering), R703.7 (stucco), R302.6 (garage separation), R302.9 (flame spread); IBC Ch. 8 (interior finishes), Ch. 25 (gypsum and plaster). Background: *Carpentry and Building Construction* (drywall, trim, flooring, paint).

## Key concepts

**Gypsum board**
- Regular: ½ in.
- Type X: ⅝ in, fire-rated.
- Moisture-resistant (green board): not allowed as a tile backer in showers or tubs under the 2018 IRC (R702.3.7).
- Shower and tub walls: use cement, fiber-cement, glass-mat gypsum or fiber-reinforced gypsum backer (R702.4.2).

**Fastener spacing (IRC Table R702.3.5), framing at 16 in o.c.**
| Location | Nails | Screws |
|---|---|---|
| Ceilings | 7 in o.c. | 12 in o.c. |
| Walls | 8 in o.c. | 16 in o.c. |

**Finish levels (GA-214)**
- Level 0: no taping.
- Level 1: tape set in compound, for plenums and above ceilings.
- Level 2: one coat over tape, for garages and tile substrate.
- Level 3: heavy texture.
- Level 4: flat paint and light texture (the most common).
- Level 5: skim coat over the whole surface, for gloss paint or critical lighting.

**Garage separation (IRC R302.6)**
- ½ in gypsum board on the garage side of walls and ceilings.
- ⅝ in Type X on the ceiling when habitable rooms are above.

**Flame spread (IBC 803.1.2, ASTM E84)**
| Class | Flame spread index | Smoke-developed |
|---|---|---|
| A | 0–25 | 450 or less |
| B | 26–75 | 450 or less |
| C | 76–200 | 450 or less |

IRC R302.9: wall and ceiling finishes max flame spread 200, smoke-developed 450.

**Plaster and stucco**
- Three-coat work: scratch coat, then brown coat, then finish coat.
- Exterior Portland cement plaster (stucco): about ⅞ in total over lath (R703.7). Moist-cure the base coats.
- Interior gypsum plaster: R702.2.

**Tile.** Thinset mortar over cement backer; or a mortar bed (mud set). Allow for movement joints.

**Paint.** Prep is everything: clean, dry, sound surface. Primer seals and gives adhesion. Latex is water-based with fast dry and low odor; alkyd/oil is solvent-based. Apply within the manufacturer's temperature and humidity ranges.

**Wood flooring.** Acclimate it on site. Leave an expansion gap at the walls (usually about ½–¾ in), hidden by base or shoe.

## Practice questions

### F1
Fire-rated Type X gypsum board is typically:
- A) ¼ in thick
- B) ⅜ in thick
- C) ½ in thick
- D) ⅝ in thick

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**D** — ⅝ in. *Ref:* IRC R302.6 / ASTM C1396

</details>

### F2
When screws attach gypsum board to wall framing at 16 in o.c., the maximum fastener spacing is:
- A) 7 in o.c.
- B) 8 in o.c.
- C) 12 in o.c.
- D) 16 in o.c.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**D** — 16 in o.c. on walls (12 in on ceilings). *Ref:* IRC Table R702.3.5

</details>

### F3
Which material is NOT permitted as a tile backer on shower walls under the 2018 IRC?
- A) Fiber-cement backer board
- B) Cement board
- C) Glass mat water-resistant gypsum backer
- D) Water-resistant gypsum board ("green board")

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**D**. *Ref:* IRC R702.3.7, R702.4.2

</details>

### F4
A Class A interior finish has a flame spread index of:
- A) 0–25
- B) 26–75
- C) 76–200
- D) 201–450

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**A**. *Ref:* IBC 803.1.2

</details>

### F5
Under the IRC, the garage side of a dwelling-garage separation wall must have at least:
- A) ½ in gypsum board
- B) ⅝ in Type X gypsum board
- C) 1-hour rated assembly
- D) ⅜ in plywood

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**A** — ½ in gypsum board (⅝ in Type X on the ceiling when habitable rooms are above). *Ref:* IRC R302.6, Table R302.6

</details>

### F6
The correct order of coats in three-coat plaster is:
- A) Brown, scratch, finish
- B) Scratch, brown, finish
- C) Finish, brown, scratch
- D) Scratch, finish, brown

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B**. *Ref:* IRC R703.7; IBC 2512

</details>

### F7
Exterior three-coat Portland cement plaster (stucco) over lath has a total thickness of about:
- A) ⅜ in
- B) ½ in
- C) ⅞ in
- D) 1½ in

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — About ⅞ in. *Ref:* IRC R703.7, Table R702.1(1)

</details>

### F8
The gypsum board finish level that calls for a skim coat over the entire surface, for gloss paint or critical lighting, is:
- A) Level 2
- B) Level 3
- C) Level 4
- D) Level 5

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**D** — Level 5. *Ref:* GA-214

</details>

### F9
Under the IRC, wall and ceiling finishes must have a flame spread index of not more than:
- A) 25
- B) 75
- C) 200
- D) 450

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 200 (smoke-developed index max 450). *Ref:* IRC R302.9

</details>

### F10
Before installing solid hardwood flooring, the installer should:
- A) Seal the subfloor with oil-based paint
- B) Acclimate the flooring and leave an expansion gap at the walls
- C) Glue it tight to the walls
- D) Install it before the HVAC is running

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B**. *Ref:* Carpentry and Building Construction

</details>
