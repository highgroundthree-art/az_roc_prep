# Carpentry — 12 items

Main source: *Carpentry and Building Construction* (2010 or 2016, **study only**). In-room backup: IRC Ch. 5 (floors), 6 (walls), 8 (roofs), R317 (decay); IBC Ch. 23 (wood).

## Key concepts

**Nominal vs actual lumber**
| Nominal | Actual |
|---|---|
| 2×4 | 1½ × 3½ |
| 2×6 | 1½ × 5½ |
| 2×8 | 1½ × 7¼ |
| 2×10 | 1½ × 9¼ |
| 2×12 | 1½ × 11¼ |
| 4×4 | 3½ × 3½ |

**Board feet** = (thickness in × width in × length ft) ÷ 12, using nominal dimensions.

**Moisture.** Grade stamp "S-DRY" means surfaced at 19% moisture content or less. Wood shrinks mostly across the grain, not along it.

**Common nails (penny)**
| Size | Length |
|---|---|
| 6d | 2 in |
| 8d | 2½ in |
| 10d | 3 in |
| 16d | 3½ in |

**Panels.** A span rating of 32/16 means max 32 in roof support spacing and 16 in floor spacing. Leave a ⅛ in gap at panel edges and ends. OSB and plywood are both rated by APA.

**Engineered wood.** LVL, PSL and glulam beams; I-joists (never cut or notch the flanges; holes go in the web only, per the manufacturer). Glulam is installed camber up.

**Framing systems.** Platform framing is standard: each floor is a platform for the next. Balloon framing has studs running continuously past the floors, so it needs fireblocking at each floor.

**Roof framing**
- Pitch is rise per 12 in of run.
- Common rafter length = √(rise² + run²).
- Ceiling joists or rafter ties resist thrust.
- Collar ties go in the upper third of the attic space.
- A ridge beam is structural; a ridge board is not.

**Stairs.** Riser count = total rise ÷ target riser height, rounded up. Tread count = risers − 1.

## Practice questions

### W1
The actual dimensions of a nominal 2×10 are:
- A) 2 × 10 in
- B) 1¾ × 9½ in
- C) 1½ × 9¼ in
- D) 1½ × 9½ in

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 1½ × 9¼ in.

</details>

### W2
How many board feet are in one 2×6 that is 12 ft long?
- A) 6
- B) 12
- C) 14
- D) 24

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — (2 × 6 × 12) ÷ 12 = 12 bd ft.

</details>

### W3
A 16d common nail is how long?
- A) 2½ in
- B) 3 in
- C) 3¼ in
- D) 3½ in

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**D** — 3½ in.

</details>

### W4
Under the IRC, foundation anchor bolts must be at least ½ in diameter, embedded at least 7 in, and spaced not more than:
- A) 4 ft o.c.
- B) 6 ft o.c.
- C) 8 ft o.c.
- D) 10 ft o.c.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — 6 ft o.c., with one bolt within 12 in of each end of each plate section. *Ref:* IRC R403.1.6

</details>

### W5
In an exterior or bearing wall, a bored hole in a single stud may not exceed what percentage of the stud width?
- A) 25%
- B) 33%
- C) 40%
- D) 60%

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 40% (up to 60% if the stud is doubled and no more than two successive studs are bored). *Ref:* IRC R602.6

</details>

### W6
In a bearing wall, a notch in a stud may not exceed:
- A) 10% of the width
- B) 25% of the width
- C) 40% of the width
- D) 1/3 of the width

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — 25%. *Ref:* IRC R602.6

</details>

### W7
Notches in sawn-lumber floor joists may not exceed 1/6 of the joist depth and may not be located:
- A) Within 2 in of the top edge
- B) In the middle third of the span
- C) Within 12 in of a bearing
- D) In the outer quarters of the span

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — The middle third of the span. *Ref:* IRC R502.8.1

</details>

### W8
Bored holes in a floor joist may not be within ___ of the top or bottom edge, and may not exceed 1/3 the joist depth.
- A) 1 in
- B) 1½ in
- C) 2 in
- D) 3 in

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 2 in. *Ref:* IRC R502.8.1

</details>

### W9
End joints in a double top plate must be offset by at least:
- A) 12 in
- B) 16 in
- C) 24 in
- D) 48 in

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 24 in. *Ref:* IRC R602.3.2

</details>

### W10
In combustible construction, fireblocking is required in concealed wall spaces at the ceiling and floor levels and at intervals not exceeding:
- A) 8 ft
- B) 10 ft
- C) 12 ft
- D) 16 ft

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — 10 ft, both vertically and horizontally. *Ref:* IRC R302.11

</details>

### W11
A structural panel stamped "32/16" may be used over roof framing spaced up to ___ and floor framing up to ___.
- A) 16 in / 32 in
- B) 32 in / 16 in
- C) 24 in / 16 in
- D) 32 in / 24 in

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — 32 in roof / 16 in floor.

</details>

### W12
In a crawl space, wood joists closer than ___ to exposed ground must be naturally durable or preservative-treated.
- A) 8 in
- B) 12 in
- C) 18 in
- D) 24 in

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 18 in (12 in for girders/beams). *Ref:* IRC R317.1

</details>

### W13
Wood framing resting on an exterior foundation wall must be treated or naturally durable when it is less than ___ from exposed earth.
- A) 6 in
- B) 8 in
- C) 12 in
- D) 18 in

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — 8 in. *Ref:* IRC R317.1

</details>

### W14
A roof has a 6-in-12 pitch and a common rafter run of 12 ft. What is the rafter's line length (no overhang)?
- A) 12.0 ft
- B) 13.4 ft
- C) 14.2 ft
- D) 18.0 ft

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — Rise = 6 ft; √(12² + 6²) = √180 = 13.42 ft.

</details>

### W15
Collar ties in a rafter-framed roof are located:
- A) At the top plate
- B) In the lower third of the attic space
- C) At mid-height of the rafter
- D) In the upper third of the attic space

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**D**. *Ref:* IRC R802.4.6

</details>

### W16
A floor-to-floor rise of 108 in with a target riser of about 7½ in requires how many risers?
- A) 13
- B) 14
- C) 15
- D) 16

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 108 ÷ 7.5 = 14.4, rounded up to 15 risers (7.2 in each, under the IRC 7¾ in max).

</details>

### W17
Lumber grade-stamped "S-DRY" was surfaced at a moisture content of:
- A) 12% or less
- B) 15% or less
- C) 19% or less
- D) 25% or less

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 19% or less.

</details>

### W18
Joist ends bearing on masonry or concrete need a minimum bearing of:
- A) 1½ in
- B) 2 in
- C) 3 in
- D) 4 in

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 3 in (1½ in on wood or metal). *Ref:* IRC R502.6

</details>
