**Construction types**
- Types I and II: noncombustible.
- Type III: noncombustible exterior walls, interior of any material.
- Type IV: heavy timber.
- Type V: any material the code allows.
- "A" means protected (rated); "B" means unprotected.

**Admin**
- A permit expires if work doesn't start within **180 days** or is suspended for 180 days (105.5).
- The special inspector is employed by the **owner or owner's agent**, not the contractor (1704.2).
- During construction, a standpipe is required before the building exceeds **40 ft** above the lowest fire department access (3311.1).

## Practice questions

### G1
Under the IBC, a permit becomes invalid if the authorized work is not commenced within:
- A) 90 days
- B) 120 days
- C) 180 days
- D) 1 year

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 180 days after issuance. *Ref:* IBC 105.5

</details>

### G2
An office building is classified as occupancy Group:
- A) A-3
- B) B
- C) M
- D) R-2

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — Business. *Ref:* IBC 304.1

</details>

### G6
In a commercial building (IBC), the required guard height at an open side of a walking surface more than 30 in above the floor below is:
- A) 34 in
- B) 36 in
- C) 38 in
- D) 42 in

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**D** — 42 in (the IRC requires 36 in). *Ref:* IBC 1015.3

### G7

- A) 2 in in diameter
- B) 4 in in diameter
- C) 4⅜ in in diameter
- D) 6 in in diameter

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — 4 in (the triangle at a stair tread and riser is 6 in). *Ref:* IBC 1015.4; IRC R312.1.3

</details>
