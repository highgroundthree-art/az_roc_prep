## Practice questions

### S1
Under 29 CFR 1926 Subpart M, fall protection is required for employees on a walking/working surface with an unprotected side or edge at what height or more?
- A) 4 ft
- B) 6 ft
- C) 10 ft
- D) 15 ft

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — 6 ft. *Ref:* 1926.501(b)(1)

</details>

### S2
The top edge height of a guardrail system's top rail must be:
- A) 36 in ± 3 in
- B) 39 in ± 2 in
- C) 42 in ± 3 in
- D) 45 in ± 3 in

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 42 in plus or minus 3 in. *Ref:* 1926.502(b)(1)

</details>

### S3
A guardrail top rail must withstand a force of at least ___ applied within 2 in of the top edge, outward or downward.
- A) 100 lb
- B) 150 lb
- C) 200 lb
- D) 300 lb

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 200 lb. *Ref:* 1926.502(b)(3)

</details>

### S4
Anchorages used for attaching personal fall arrest equipment must support at least ___ per employee attached, unless designed by a qualified person with a 2:1 safety factor.
- A) 1,800 lb
- B) 3,000 lb
- C) 5,000 lb
- D) 10,000 lb

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 5,000 lb. *Ref:* 1926.502(d)(15)

</details>

### S5
An excavation requires a protective system (sloping, shoring or shielding) when it is ___ or deeper, unless made entirely in stable rock.
- A) 4 ft
- B) 5 ft
- C) 6 ft
- D) 8 ft

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — 5 ft. *Ref:* 1926.652(a)(1)

</details>

### S6
In a trench 4 ft or more deep, a stairway, ladder, ramp or other safe means of egress must be located so lateral travel to it is no more than:
- A) 15 ft
- B) 25 ft
- C) 50 ft
- D) 75 ft

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — 25 ft. *Ref:* 1926.651(c)(2)

</details>

### S7
Excavated material (spoil) must be kept back from the edge of the excavation at least:
- A) 1 ft
- B) 2 ft
- C) 3 ft
- D) 5 ft

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — 2 ft, or retained by a device that prevents it falling in. *Ref:* 1926.651(j)(2)

</details>

### S8
The maximum allowable slope for an excavation less than 20 ft deep in Type C soil is:
- A) ¾H:1V
- B) 1H:1V
- C) 1½H:1V
- D) 2H:1V

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 1½ horizontal to 1 vertical (34°). *Ref:* 1926 Subpart P, Appendix B, Table B-1

</details>

### S9
Type A soil has an unconfined compressive strength of:
- A) 0.5 tsf or less
- B) 0.5 to 1.0 tsf
- C) 1.0 to 1.5 tsf
- D) 1.5 tsf or greater

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**D** — 1.5 tons per square foot or greater. *Ref:* 1926 Subpart P, Appendix A

</details>

### S10
Protective systems for excavations deeper than ___ must be designed by a registered professional engineer.
- A) 10 ft
- B) 15 ft
- C) 20 ft
- D) 25 ft

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 20 ft. *Ref:* 1926.652(b) and (c)

</details>

### S11
Who must inspect excavations, adjacent areas and protective systems daily and before the start of each shift?
- A) The project owner
- B) A registered professional engineer
- C) A competent person
- D) The building official

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — A competent person. *Ref:* 1926.651(k)(1)

</details>

### S12
When a portable ladder is used to access an upper landing, its side rails must extend at least ___ above the landing.
- A) 2 ft
- B) 3 ft
- C) 4 ft
- D) 5 ft

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — 3 ft. *Ref:* 1926.1053(b)(1)

</details>

### S13
A non-self-supporting ladder with a 16 ft working length should have its base set out from the wall:
- A) 2 ft
- B) 3 ft
- C) 4 ft
- D) 5 ft

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 4 ft (1 ft horizontal per 4 ft of working length). *Ref:* 1926.1053(b)(5)(i)

</details>

### S14
A stairway or ladder must be provided at every point of access where there is a break in elevation of ___ or more and no ramp, runway or personnel hoist.
- A) 12 in
- B) 19 in
- C) 24 in
- D) 30 in

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — 19 in. *Ref:* 1926.1051(a)

</details>

### S15
Each scaffold and scaffold component must support, without failure, its own weight and at least ___ the maximum intended load.
- A) 2 times
- B) 3 times
- C) 4 times
- D) 5 times

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 4 times. *Ref:* 1926.451(a)(1)

</details>

### S16
Employees on a scaffold must be protected from falls when the platform is more than ___ above a lower level.
- A) 6 ft
- B) 8 ft
- C) 10 ft
- D) 15 ft

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 10 ft. *Ref:* 1926.451(g)(1)

</details>

### S17
When a masonry wall is being built, a limited access zone must be established. Its width is:
- A) Equal to the wall height
- B) Wall height plus 2 ft
- C) Wall height plus 4 ft
- D) Twice the wall height

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — Height of the wall plus 4 ft, running the entire length of the wall. *Ref:* 1926.706(a)

</details>

### S18
During steel erection, workers other than connectors and those in a controlled decking zone must be protected from falls at heights over:
- A) 6 ft
- B) 10 ft
- C) 15 ft
- D) 30 ft

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 15 ft. *Ref:* 1926.760(a)(1)

</details>

### S19
All columns must be anchored by a minimum of ___ anchor rods (anchor bolts).
- A) 2
- B) 3
- C) 4
- D) 6

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 4. *Ref:* 1926.755(a)(1)

</details>

### S20
On a construction site, 120-volt, single-phase, 15- and 20-amp receptacles that are not part of the permanent wiring must be protected by GFCIs or by:
- A) A lockout/tagout program
- B) An assured equipment grounding conductor program
- C) Weatherproof covers
- D) A licensed electrician's daily inspection

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B** — An assured equipment grounding conductor program. *Ref:* 1926.404(b)(1)

</details>

### S21
A fire extinguisher rated not less than 2A must be provided for each ___ of protected building area, with travel distance not exceeding 100 ft.
- A) 1,500 sq ft
- B) 2,500 sq ft
- C) 3,000 sq ft
- D) 5,000 sq ft

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 3,000 sq ft. *Ref:* 1926.150(c)(1)(i)

</details>

### S22
A personal fall arrest system must be rigged so an employee can neither free fall more than ___ nor contact any lower level.
- A) 3½ ft
- B) 4 ft
- C) 6 ft

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C** — 6 ft. *Ref:* 1926.502(d)(16)(iii)
