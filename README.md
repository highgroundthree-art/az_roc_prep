# AZ ROC Prep — KB-2 Dual Residential & Small Commercial

Study system for the Arizona Registrar of Contractors (AZ ROC) license exams required for the **KB-2 Dual Residential and Small Commercial** classification.

## What has to be passed

| Exam | Delivered by | Format | Pass | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Combined B-1 / B-2 / KB-1 / KB-2 Trade Exam | PSI Services | 100 questions, 240 min, open book (3 references) | 70% | Can be replaced by NASCLA (below) |
| Statutes and Rules Exam (SRE) | AZ ROC via Gmetrix (online only) | Open-book training course + exam | 70% | Required, cannot be waived. $61 |
| *Alternative:* NASCLA Commercial General Building | PSI / NASCLA | 115 questions, 330 min | 81 correct | Accepted in lieu of the AZ trade exam for KB-2. $106 + $25 NASCLA app fee |
